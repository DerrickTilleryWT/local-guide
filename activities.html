Explore Downtown Fun

Raleigh offers incredible hands-on experiences for children to learn, play, and burn off energy.

Marbles Kids Museum

Located right in downtown Raleigh, Marbles Kids Museum is designed specifically for children under 10. It is a completely hands-on environment where kids are encouraged to touch, play, and explore.

Museum Highlights

  • Around Town: A kid-sized version of a city where children can pretend to be mechanics, chefs, or EMTs.
  • Splash!: A massive indoor water play area (waterproof aprons provided).
  • STEM Play: Interactive exhibits focusing on math, science, and building.
  • IMAX Theater: Watch 3D educational films on North Carolina's only 3D-capable giant screen.
