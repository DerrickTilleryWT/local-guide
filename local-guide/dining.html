Eats for Everyone

Finding a spot where the whole family is happy doesn't have to be a challenge. Raleigh has plenty of diverse, kid-friendly dining options.

Morgan Street Food Hall

Dining out with young kids can be tough when everyone wants something different. Morgan Street Food Hall solves this by housing dozens of different local food vendors in one loud, casual, family-friendly building.

What to Expect

  • Kid-friendly options: Wood-fired pizza, burgers, and rolled ice cream.
  • Adult options: Craft tacos, fresh sushi, curries, and a full bar.
  • Seating: Plenty of large communal tables and a spacious outdoor patio perfect for noisy families.
